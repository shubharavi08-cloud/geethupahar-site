Refund & Correction Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Because every song is custom-made for you, we cannot offer returns once it is delivered. We want you to love your song, so we offer the following.

Free correction

If a name is mispronounced, a detail you gave is wrong in the lyrics or video, or there is a technical problem, tell us from your order page within 7 days of delivery. We will fix it once, free of charge.

Refund

  • If we cannot deliver within 7 days of payment, you can ask for a full refund.
  • If, after one correction, you are still not satisfied because we did not follow your instructions, we will refund the order.
  • If you cancel before we start the song, you get a full refund.

Not eligible

Change of mind after delivery, or requests that differ from the story and details you submitted.

How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the original payment method through Razorpay and usually reach you within 5–7 working days.
