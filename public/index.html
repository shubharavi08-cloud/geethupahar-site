Questions

 

Who creates the song?

Your song is created with the help of AI music tools and finished by our team. We do not imitate any real singer's voice.

How long does it take?

Delivered within 48 hours of payment. You get a WhatsApp message with your private link.

Can you make a song for my Reel, business or channel?

Yes. Choose "Reel / social media" when you order, tell us your name, brand or story, and we make a short catchy song for Reels, Shorts and Status.

Can you make a devotional song for a festival or pooja?

Yes — bhajan and aarti-style songs with your family's names and occasion, for festivals, griha pravesh, naming ceremonies and poojas. Choose "Devotional" when you order.

What if a name is pronounced wrong?

Tell us how to say it when you order. If anything is off, we fix it once for free — see our refund & correction policy.

Are my photos safe?

Photos are used only to make your video, kept private, and deleted from our system 30 days after delivery. See our privacy policy.

Which languages?

Hindi, Kannada, Tamil, Telugu and English. Choose when you order.

How do I pay?

UPI, cards, netbanking and wallets — securely through Razorpay.
