Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

respects your privacy and follows the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

What we collect

  • Your name, WhatsApp number and (optional) email — to deliver your order and support you.
  • The story and names you write — to compose your song.
  • Photos you upload (video plan) — only to make your video.
  • Payment status from Razorpay — we do not receive your card or UPI details.

How we use it

Only to create and deliver your order, answer your queries, and fix any issues. We do not sell your data and we do not use your photos or story for advertising or to train any AI model.

Who sees it

Our small team, and service providers needed to run the site (hosting and Razorpay for payments). Your order link is private and unguessable; anyone you share it with can view your song.

How long we keep it

  • Photos: deleted 30 days after delivery.
  • Finished song and video: kept for 180 days so you can download again, then deleted.
  • Unpaid orders and their photos: deleted after 3 days.

Your rights

You can ask us to correct or delete your data at any time by messaging us on WhatsApp or email; we will act within a reasonable time.
